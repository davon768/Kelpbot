Better with friends

Games the whole channel plays

💰 Heists /heist

Start a heist and your friends buy in within 60 seconds. A crew of two has a 50% chance; five or more have 80%, but split the loot more ways.

🏇 Horse races /race

The first bet opens the betting window. Then everyone watches the race play out live, from Seaweed Sprinter at 2.7x to Lucky Limpet at 11.8x.

⚔️ Duels /duel

Challenge someone. You both put up the same bet, a coin decides, and the winner takes it all.

🎟️ Lottery /lottery

Tickets go into one pot for the server, and the draw happens 24 hours after the first ticket is bought. More tickets mean better odds.

For server owners

Your server, your rules

  • A separate economy for every serverBalances, items and leaderboards never cross between servers.
  • Tune everythingSet the currency name and emoji, starting money, pay, bet limits and interest, and turn any game on or off.
  • A tidy channelReplies clean themselves up after two minutes, and errors are only visible to the person who caused them.
  • Events and seasonsRun Double Pay or Lucky Hour for an evening. End a season to crown the top three and start everyone fresh.
  • Built to be fairBrand-new accounts can't funnel money, bets come back if the bot restarts, and a log channel records big wins and admin actions.
